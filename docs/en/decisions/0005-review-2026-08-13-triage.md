# 0005: Usefulness triage and rejected items from the 2026-08-13 evaluation review

## Status

Accepted. Triage of the findings is complete, and there are no further changes to follow (if this triage is ever overturned, this ADR should be marked Superseded and a new ADR with a new number should be started).

## Background

For `テスト知識管理のGit-nativeモデル_評価レビュー.md` (2026-08-13 edition, a revised version re-evaluating the response status to the initial 2026-08-12 review), each finding was judged as useful/not useful in `テスト知識管理のGit-nativeモデル_評価レビュー_有用性判定と修正指示.md`. The judgment criterion had two axes: whether it directly improves the accuracy/reliability/usability of the markharness tool's functionality, behavior, or documentation (useful), or whether it only serves to satisfy a reviewer (defending novelty, the form of an empirical study) without changing the tool itself (not useful). "Not useful" does not mean the finding is factually wrong; it means it does not contribute to this project's purpose (examining product design).

The response to items judged useful (decomposing the branch-strategy claim, a JSON Schema for `executions/*/results.yml`, explicitly documenting ID immutability, cleaning up the knowledge-graph representation, the related-work comparison, weakening the large-scale backfill claim) has already been reflected in the changelog of `テスト知識管理のGit-nativeモデル_統合版.md` (2026-08-13(3) onward), [decisions/0004](./0004-feature-id-change-migration.md), and `src/schema.rs`/`src/validate.rs`.

Since the response is complete, the evaluation review body (`テスト知識管理のGit-nativeモデル_評価レビュー.md`), the usefulness-triage document (`テスト知識管理のGit-nativeモデル_評価レビュー_有用性判定と修正指示.md`), and the execution prompt collection (`improvement-prompts.md`; for the response to the 2026-08-12 review, items 1–6 and 11 are complete, and items 7–10 are rejected as described below) have all served their purpose, so their key points are transcribed into this decision record and the files are deleted.

## Rejected items (judged not useful, no action taken) and their reasons

- **Refining/pre-registering the within-subjects experimental protocol** (sample-size power calculation, within-subjects crossover, pre-registration of multiple comparisons): This is a procedure for academically substantiating RQ1 (Chapter 5) and has no bearing on the tool's design. Confirming whether the tool is actually useful is achieved more cheaply and directly through dogfooding in real operation and continuous feedback from users than through formal statistical verification.
- **Tightening the procedure for building the co-change oracle** (final oracle determination by multiple independent raters, pre-registered disagreement-resolution rules): Same reason as above. This is meaningless unless the subject experiment itself is actually conducted.
- **Changing the paper title** (e.g., "A Version-Aware Knowledge Graph Model" → "Git-native ChangeEvent Model," etc.): This is a matter of framing/presentation and has no impact on the product design itself. That `derived_from` is a series of diff events rather than a persistent graph is already made explicit not in the title but in the explanations in README, cli-manual.md, and the body of 統合版.md ([decision 0001](./0001-version-dag-to-changeevent-model.md)).
- **Implementing an existing-TMS importer** (part of `improvement-prompts.md` item 7): Already recorded as unimplemented in `docs/README.md` (former) and Chapter 7 Future Work of 統合版.md as UC8; work will not begin until a concrete request emerges.
